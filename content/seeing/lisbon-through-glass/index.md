They say one of the hardest cities to photograph is the one you are currently living in. You have grown accustomed to a certain light, certain details, even certain faces — you stop realising what makes it unique.

During a photography workshop, we were challenged to take it on. These are not comprehensive. Just some — hopefully different — perspectives on this highly instagrammable town.
